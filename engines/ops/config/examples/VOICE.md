# Editorial voice

Write clear Turkish with correct characters. Use concrete, sourced statements.
Avoid exaggerated promises, invented opinions, private customer names and personal
data. Separate evidence from a suggestion. Ask the owner when an opinion is missing.

| Platform | Length | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | 80–200 words | One sourced point; no invented performance claims. |
| X | At most 280 characters | One concise point; approved threads use 3–6 posts. |

These are editable editorial choices, subject to the job's hard output limits.
