# Security policy

Do not report exploitable vulnerabilities, credentials or personal meeting data in
public issues. Use GitHub's private reporting instead: open the repository's
**Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Include a minimal
description, the affected version or commit, impact and safe reproduction steps.

Avoid live exploitation, disruption or accessing other people's data. Redact tokens,
tenant/customer IDs and transcripts. Maintainers should coordinate a fix and
disclosure privately. See [security model](docs/security.md). Support versions and
response targets are TODO until a release-maintenance policy is adopted.
